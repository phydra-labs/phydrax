# Variational molecular kinetics

PhydraX fits slow coordinates and finite-state kinetics from `TrajectoryData` without
crossing padding or reset boundaries. Estimation, neural representation learning,
state discretization, and validation remain separate contracts.

## Lagged evidence

`fit_vamp`, `fit_vac`, and `fit_tica` consume a positive integer lag and an explicit
`LaggedPairWeighting`:

- `UNIFORM` gives every valid transition equal mass;
- `SOURCE` uses the time-origin sample weight;
- `GEOMETRIC` uses the geometric endpoint evidence already carried by
  `TrajectoryTransitions`.

The result records valid/excluded pairs, effective sample size, source/target mean
mismatch, and the minimum, maximum, and mean physical lag. A fit may not report
physical implied timescales unless all valid pairs share one positive physical lag
within tolerance.

`fit_vamp` returns separate left and right canonical functions. Its score is the
centered, nontrivial VAMP-2 score; it does not silently insert a stationary constant
mode. `fit_vac` symmetrizes equilibrium covariance evidence and solves a generalized
self-adjoint problem. `fit_tica` is the raw-state linear specialization of `fit_vac`.
Repeated eigenvalues leave a valid slow subspace but make an individual basis
non-identifiable.

## Markov-state models

`fit_markov_state_model` accepts either integer state labels or soft simplex
assignments aligned with the complete trajectory capacity. It constructs counts only
from valid lagged pairs. A reversible model symmetrizes counts before row
normalization and reports detailed-balance error, empty states, communicating classes,
and stationary residual.

Use `validate_markov_models(short, long, multiplier)` to compare an independently
fitted long-lag matrix with the corresponding power of a short-lag matrix. This is a
Chapman--Kolmogorov diagnostic, not an automatic proof of Markovianity.

## Learned slow coordinates

`fit_variational_kinetic_model` trains a deterministic `AbstractArrayModel` against
the exact empirical global covariance objective. The first execution contract is
bounded full-batch training. It rejects a transition set larger than
`maximum_transitions`; it never substitutes independent minibatch VAMP losses.

The selected `VariationalCoordinateModel` includes the final canonical rotation and
is directly usable as a model-backed collective variable. Checkpoint resume requires
exact model, data, feature, lag, optimizer, and policy identities.

For atomistic trajectories, construct a `CollectiveVariableFeatureLibrary` from a
prepared dynamics runtime and a CV program. This gives the kinetic estimator
translation/rotation/permutation behavior inherited from the authored CVs rather than
from flattened Cartesian coordinates.

## Biased trajectories

Configuration weights can correct thermodynamic averages under their stated measure.
They do not generally recover unbiased kinetics from a trajectory generated by a
different biased dynamics. A kinetic claim from biased trajectories requires an
appropriate path or dynamical reweighting derivation outside the configuration-weight
contract.
