# Accelerator beam dynamics

`phydrax.applications.accelerator` provides a fixed-capacity reference beamline profile and provider boundaries for broader accelerator production.

## Convention and bunch

`AcceleratorConvention` fixes the coordinate order `(x, px/p0, y, py/p0, zeta, delta)`, longitudinal sign, and momentum normalization. The reference particle is separate from every macroparticle. `AcceleratorBunch` stores stable IDs, weights, activity, reference rest energy, reference momentum, and reference charge.

## Beamline

`BeamlinePlan` lowers a fixed sequence of drifts, split-map quadrupoles, steerers, thin RF gaps, circular apertures, and sector-bend references into `lax.scan`. `track_beamline` returns every element state, activity history, first loss element, finite-step evidence, and the accepted final bunch. Capacity and aperture losses are explicit; no particle is silently discarded.

`beam_diagnostics` returns weighted six-dimensional moments, transverse geometric emittances, Twiss alpha/beta, and transmission.

## Collective physics

`SpaceChargeKickPlan` applies a kick only when an external or existing-PIC field solve carries exact source, frame transform, boundary condition, and residual evidence. It does not relabel PIC as a universal accelerator collective solver.

`SpaceChargeIGFPlan(scale, grid, capacity=..., minimum_cells_per_sigma=3.0)` is the native rest-frame space-charge route and is distinct from the provider kick above: it owns its field solve. Each `kick(bunch, step_length, frame_lorentz_factor=None)` (and its traceable `evaluate`):

1. reads the reference kinematics (`reference_rest_energy` and `reference_momentum = p₀c` in the energy unit of the bound `ElectromagneticScaleContract`, positions in its length unit, `reference_charge` in elementary charges) and boosts every macroparticle proper velocity into the rest frame `γ₀` (the reference, or `frame_lorentz_factor` for a quasi-static frame such as the mean longitudinal motion `γ_z = γ/√(1 + a_w²)` in an undulator, where the coordinates must describe that mean motion) with `phydrax.boost_proper_velocity`; the largest rest-frame `|β'|` is reported as `maximum_rest_frame_speed` and must stay below the plan bound (default `0.3`, which caps the neglected rest-frame magnetic force at `β'² ≲ 0.1`);
2. deposits macro-charge with multilinear assignment onto `grid` in centroid-relative rest-frame coordinates `(x − x̄, y − ȳ, γ₀(z − z̄))`, where `z = −zeta` for the `"positive-late"` convention (`+zeta` for `"positive-early"`), so `grid` bounds are rest-frame extents around the charge centroid and the longitudinal extent must cover `γ₀` times the lab bunch length plus one cell of assignment support at each end;
3. solves the open-boundary Poisson equation with the cell-integrated Coulomb kernel and its face-difference field kernel (`FreeSpaceConvolutionPlan("coulomb-igf", grid, gradient=True)`, Qiang et al. 2006), giving `E'` without finite differences and second-order accurate at any cell aspect ratio;
4. transforms `(E', 0)` back to the lab frame with `phydrax.boost_fields` (so `E⊥ = γ₀E'⊥`, `E_z = E'_z`, `B = v₀ × E / c²`), gathers `E + v × B` on the particles, and applies the exact three-momentum increment `Δ(pc) = q (E + v × B) Δs / β_z`, updating `px/p0`, `py/p0`, and `delta` consistently.

`SpaceChargeIGFResult` carries the rest-frame potential and field, the lab fields, the deposited charge density, deposited versus total charge, per-particle `support`, `cells_per_sigma` (the charge-weighted rest-frame rms bunch size per grid axis in cells), `resolved`, `finite`, and `accepted`. Deposit and gather each smooth the bunch over one cell, so the field error is set by `cells_per_sigma`: for a Gaussian bunch the longitudinal field at the particles is ≈ 4 % low at 4 cells per σ, 7 % at 3, 15 % at 2, and 45 % at 1, independent of the cell aspect ratio (1 to 1000). The bunch is updated only when every active particle is inside the grid with full assignment support, every axis reaches `minimum_cells_per_sigma`, the deposit and solve are finite, the frame is valid, and the rest-frame speed bound holds; otherwise the returned bunch is unchanged and `accepted` is false.

## Wakefields and impedance

`WakeFunctionPlan(kind, zeta_samples, wake_values, *, units, causality_convention)` tabulates one causal wake in explicit SI units. `kind` is a `WakeKind`: `"longitudinal"` (units `"V/C"`), or `"dipolar-x"`, `"dipolar-y"`, `"quadrupolar-x"`, `"quadrupolar-y"` (units `"V/C/m"`); a units selector that does not match the kind is refused. `causality_convention="behind-positive"` tabulates the distance behind the source (`zeta ≥ 0`), `"behind-negative"` accepts Chao's `z ≤ 0` form and is negated on entry. Tables start at zero distance, the wake is zero ahead of the source, and it is treated as zero beyond the last sample. Transverse tables must vanish at zero distance (Panofsky–Wenzel). The value at zero is the right limit `W∥(0⁺)`; a slice interacts with itself through `W∥(0⁺)/2` (fundamental theorem of beam loading), so a point bunch of charge `Q` loses `Q²·k` with loss factor `k = W∥(0⁺)/2`.

`apply_wake(plan, bunch, scale, *, bin_count, arrival_time=0.0, memory=None)` binds an `ElectromagneticScaleContract`: bunch reference energy, momentum, and charge are in its units, `zeta` and transverse offsets in its length unit, and each macroparticle carries charge `weight × reference_charge`. Active, valid particles are binned by `zeta` into `bin_count` equal bins whose centers span the bunch; `AcceleratorConvention.longitudinal_sign` decides which direction is behind (`"positive-late"`: larger `zeta` is behind). The single-passage potential is the causal convolution (`phydrax.signal.convolve`) of the binned source with the wake sampled at bin lags:

- longitudinal: source is the bin charge; every particle in a bin changes energy by `ΔE = −q V`, applied to `delta` as `Δδ = ΔE·E/(p₀c)²`;
- dipolar: source is the charge-weighted *source* offset `Σ q x_s`; the witness receives `Δp⊥c = q V` independent of its own offset;
- quadrupolar: source is the bin charge; the witness receives `Δp⊥c = q x_w V`, proportional to its *own* offset.

`WakeKickResult` carries the kicked bunch, bin edges and width, line density, total and single-passage potentials, per-particle kicks, and evidence: the bunch `energy_change` (J) and `loss_factor = −energy_change/Q²` (V/C) for longitudinal wakes, `momentum_kick_sum` for transverse wakes, `causality_defect` (largest self potential ahead of the first charged bin), `kernel_truncated` (bunch longer than the table), `history_sufficient`, and `finite`. The kicked bunch is returned unchanged when the result is not finite.

Multi-bunch and multi-turn wakes use `WakeMemoryState.empty(history_length, bin_count)`, a bounded ring of recorded passages (binned charge, dipole moments, and absolute positions `c·t_arrival + zeta`). `record_passage(memory, bunch, scale, arrival_time=...)` stores a passage, overwriting the oldest once full; `apply_wake(..., memory=memory, arrival_time=...)` adds the wake of every stored passage behind which the witness bins sit. `history_sufficient` is false when a passage has been dropped while the oldest stored passage is still inside the tabulated wake extent. Both functions trace inside `lax.scan`, so coupled-bunch tracking can carry the memory through turns.

### Impedance duality

With phasors `exp(−iωt)` and `τ = zeta/c`, `Z∥(ω) = ∫₀^∞ W∥(τ) e^{iωτ} dτ` (Ω) and `Z⊥(ω) = −i ∫₀^∞ W⊥(τ) e^{iωτ} dτ` (Ω/m); frequencies are in hertz. `wake_impedance(plan, frequencies, *, scale, tolerance)` evaluates the trapezoid sum over the table with the gridded Type-3 nonuniform Fourier transform and reports `phase_step = |ω|·Δτ_max`; `supported` requires `phase_step ≤ π` and the target inside the transform box. `wake_from_impedance(kind, frequencies, impedance, zeta_samples, *, scale, tolerance)` inverts `W(τ) = (1/π) Re ∫₀^∞ Z̃(ω) e^{−iωτ} dω` (`Z̃ = Z∥` or `iZ⊥`) over the supplied band and returns a `WakeFunctionPlan`; its `origin_value` is the raw inverse at zero distance, the midpoint `W∥(0⁺)/2` of the causal jump.

### Wake models

`ResonatorWake(shunt_impedance, quality, resonant_frequency, *, kind, scale)` is the broadband resonator (`Q > 1/2`). With `α = ω_r/(2Q)` and `ω̄ = √(ω_r² − α²)`, `W∥(τ) = (ω_r R_s/Q) e^{−ατ}[cos ω̄τ − (α/ω̄) sin ω̄τ]`, `W⊥(τ) = (ω_r² R⊥/(Q ω̄)) e^{−ατ} sin ω̄τ`, `Z∥ = R_s/(1 + iQ(ω_r/ω − ω/ω_r))`, and `Z⊥ = (ω_r/ω) R⊥/(1 + iQ(ω_r/ω − ω/ω_r))`. `loss_factor` is `ω_r R_s/(2Q)`; `wake_function(zeta_samples)` tabulates a plan and `impedance(frequencies)` evaluates the closed form.

`ResistiveWallWake(radius, conductivity, length, *, scale)` is the round-pipe DC-conductivity resistive wall of Bane and Sands (SLAC-PUB-95-7074), with characteristic length `s₀ = (2a²/(Z₀σ))^{1/3}`. `wake_function(kind, zeta_samples)` evaluates the full short-range formulas (longitudinal `W(0⁺) = Z₀cL/(πa²)`; dipolar vanishing at zero) with their integral by Gauss–Legendre quadrature up to `50 s₀`, and the classical long-range forms `−(cL/(4πa))√(Z₀/(πσ)) s^{−3/2}` and `(cL/(πa³))√(Z₀/(πσ)) s^{−1/2}` beyond it; the relative mismatch of the two forms at the crossover is recorded. A round pipe has no quadrupolar wake, so that kind is refused.

Beam-beam effects, collimation scattering, high-order maps, and machine correction remain separately qualified native or external provider capabilities. Coherent synchrotron radiation and incoherent storage-ring radiation damping and quantum excitation are covered below.

## Coherent synchrotron radiation

`CSRLattice(lengths, curvatures, *, element_ids, entrance_edges=None, exit_edges=None)` is a planar path of drifts and circular arcs. Positive curvature `h = 1/ρ` bends the orbit toward `−x`, so `+x` points away from the center of curvature. Pole-face angles are thin hard-edge focusing kicks (`Δpx = h tan(e) x`, `Δpy = −h tan(e) y`); a rectangular bend of angle `θ` has `θ` on one face and zero on the other when its neighbors are drifts, as in a chicane. Before the lattice the path continues as a straight line along the entrance direction, after it along the exit direction.

`CSRPlan(model, lattice, scale, grid, *, reference_rest_energy, reference_momentum, capacity, ...)` binds one `CSRModel`:

| Model | Physics | Grid |
|---|---|---|
| `"1d-steady"` | Saldin–Derbenev steady wake `W(z) = −(2q/4πε₀)(3R²)^{−1/3} ∫ (z − z′)^{−1/3} λ′(z′) dz′` with the kernel integrated exactly over cells; zero in drifts | `(z,)` |
| `"1d-transient-shielded"` | Exact 1-D retarded line charge on the lattice (Mayes and Hoffstaetter 2009): entrance and exit transients, previous bends and drifts, density history (bunch compression), and with `plate_gap` the image series `2Σ(−1)ⁿ` of parallel plates | `(z,)` |
| `"3d-steady-igf"` | Steady Green functions of a source on the orbit (Cai–Ding 2020 longitudinal potential with its Coulomb term; exact-Lorentz transverse potentials `ψ̂_x`, `ψ_y`), cell-integrated and convolved on the doubled grid of `FreeSpaceConvolutionPlan("tabulated", ...)` | `(x, y, z)` |
| `"3d-retarded-mesh"` | Retarded Liénard–Wiechert potentials of the smooth deposited density over its recorded history | `(x, y, z)` |

Grids are uniform cell-centered bunch-frame grids relative to the reference particle; `z` points ahead (`z = −zeta` for `"positive-late"`). Macro-charge is deposited with multilinear assignment and smoothed with a Gaussian of width `smoothing` per axis (the kernel width of the retarded mesh). Wakes are energy changes per unit length of a reference-charge particle; kicks apply `Δδ = ΔE·E₀/(p₀c)²` and `Δ(p⊥/p₀) = F⊥Δs/(β₀p₀c)`.

Retarded models write `dE/ds = −dΦ/ds|particle + W_rad`, with the Liénard–Wiechert potential `Φ` and `W_rad = (q/4πε₀)∫(β²u·u′ − 1)∂λ/∂z′ ds′/R`. The field of the same density moving on a straight line (space charge, owned by `SpaceChargeIGFPlan`) is subtracted pairwise, which removes the line-charge singularity. The full part is integrated over the path lag with the Jefimenko measure `ds′/R` on nodes that resolve both the density (retarded lags of fixed offsets, found with the native bracketed `scalar_root`) and the far-upstream horizon where `dΔ/dv → 0`; the straight-line part is integrated over the retarded offset. Tracking stores each particle's potential energy and applies `−ΔqΦ` between kicks, so the potential term telescopes exactly and includes the path-length slip of off-axis particles. The 3-D mesh forces use `Ψ = Φ − βcA_s`: `F_x = −q(∂_xΨ + hΨ) − q c dA_x/dt` (Lorentz force plus the curvilinear `−qφ/ρ` term) and `F_y = −q∂_yΨ`. The IGF route integrates the same exact Lorentz force, written as closed-form elliptic antiderivatives in the retarded half-angle, so both 3-D routes give the same steady forces: the residual centripetal force of a Gaussian bunch is `F_x = −2qλ(z)/(4πε₀ρ)` for any transverse aspect ratio (Derbenev and Shiltsev 1996; Stupakov, PRAB 25, 014401, 2022). Cai and Ding's published transverse potentials (PRAB 23, 014402, 2020) are not used: their paraxial step `β_s ≈ β` moves the test particle at `β(1 + x/ρ)c`, and the resulting `−βχB` acting on the near Coulomb field raises the coefficient to `2 + 2⟨x²/r²⟩` (3 for a round beam).

`CSRPlan.wake(density, *, position, reference_charge, state=None, derivative_step=None)` returns a `CSRWake` for a given charge density (frozen history without `state`); retarded models need `derivative_step` for `dΦ/ds`. `initial_state(bunch, position=...)` seeds a `CSRState` (bounded density ring, incoming-drift snapshot, per-particle potential memory) and `kick(bunch, state, position=..., length=...)` returns a `CSRKickResult` with per-particle kicks, energy and transverse-impulse ledgers, and `CSREvidence`. `CSRTrackingPlan(plan, substeps=...)` and `track_csr(tracking, bunch)` integrate CSR into split-step first-order tracking (edge kick, half map, CSR kick at the step midpoint, half map, edge kick) inside one `lax.scan`, with per-step ledgers and status.

`CSREvidence.status` holds `CSRStatus` bits. Refusals leave the bunch unkicked: `NONFINITE`, `UNSUPPORTED` (charge outside the grid), `ROOT_FAILURE` (a retarded-time solve did not converge), `HISTORY_INCOMPLETE` (a retarded time fell into history dropped from the `history_capacity` ring), and `SHIELDING_TRUNCATED` (the half-weighted last image pair exceeds `shielding_tolerance`). Validity bits are reported: `DERBENEV_VIOLATED` (1-D models with `σ_x/(σ_z²R)^{1/3} ≥ derbenev_limit`) and `NOT_STEADY` (steady models inside the overtaking length `(24σ_zR²)^{1/3}` of the current arc). `CSRResources` bounds retarded solves, history bytes, and kernel bytes per evaluation; `CSRPlan.estimate` reports them and construction raises `CSRResourceError` above the limits. The 3-D IGF plan reports `kernel_defect`, the change of the near-origin kernel under doubled transverse quadrature.

External oracles run through pinned interpreters only: `ocelot_csr_tracking(OcelotCSRProvider(python), lattice, bunch, ...)` tracks with Ocelot's 1-D transient CSR, and `pycsr3d_longitudinal_wake(PyCSR3DProvider(python), plan, density, curvature=...)` evaluates PyCSR3D's steady wake on the plan grid.

elegant (Borland, APS; EPICS Open License) is a pinned-binary oracle for the 1-D models: `run_elegant_csr(ElegantCSRProvider(pin_executable(elegant, ...)), tracking, bunch, destination)` writes `csr.ele`, `csr.lte`, and a binary SDDS `beam.sdds` from the `CSRTrackingPlan` and electron `AcceleratorBunch` (`elegant_csr_input`: bends → `CSRCSBEND` with the plan's angles, hard-edge pole faces, and substeps as `N_SLICES`; drifts → `CSRDRIFT`, Stupakov exit transient for `"1d-transient-shielded"` and `CSR=0` for `"1d-steady"`; `BINS` matching the plan cell; smoothing as a variance-matched Savitzky–Golay moving average; `x′ = (px/p₀)/p_z`, `t = σζ/(β₀c)`, `p = (1+δ)β₀γ₀`), and reads the final particle SDDS artifact back into accelerator coordinates (`elegant_csr_bunch`). `ElegantCSRResult` carries the tracked bunch, the provider version, executable digest, license, output digest, and an `AdapterReport` whose losses name the transformed binning, transport order, smoothing, and (transient) CSR model. Parallel-plate shielding, 3-D models, non-electron bunches, and unequal macroparticle weights are refused. Live tests read `PHYDRAX_ELEGANT` and `PHYDRAX_ELEGANT_VERSION`.

Nonclaims: no shielding in the 3-D routes, no side walls, no transverse velocity or energy dependence of the retarded source motion (sources follow the reference path at the reference speed), no microbunching-instability gain analysis, and no second-order transport inside `track_csr`.

## Field maps and insertion devices

Magnet elements implement the core `phydrax.discretization.pic.ExternalFieldSource` capability: `external_fields(positions[N, 3], times[N])` returns an `ExternalFieldSample` with `E`, `B`, and a per-sample `support` mask. Unsupported samples carry zero field and are refused by every consumer; they are never treated as field-free space. Elements live in one straight Cartesian map frame: `z` is the beamline axis `s`, `(x, y)` are horizontal and vertical offsets.

Analytic magnets are exact vacuum magnetostatic fields. An analytic on-axis profile `b(ζ)` gives `B_y = Re b(z + i y)` and `B_z = Im b(z + i y)` (and a horizontal profile `h` adds `B_x = Re h(z + i x)`, `B_z += Im h(z + i x)`), which is curl- and divergence-free wherever `b` is analytic. Longitudinal envelopes use the window `g(u) = ½[tanh((u + a)/w) − tanh((u − a)/w)]`, whose integral is exactly `2a`; its poles at `Im u = ±πw/2` limit the aperture in each analytic plane to `πw/4`, and a larger aperture is refused at construction.

- `InsertionDeviceField(peak_field, period, period_count, *, polarization, center, aperture, ramp_periods=1.0)` is a planar (`b = B₀ g cos k_u u`) or helical (adds `h = −b′/k_u`, a constant-magnitude rotating on-axis field) undulator; a wiggler is the same device at `K ≫ 1`. The flat top holds exactly `period_count` periods and the ramps have width `ramp_periods · λ_u`. Terminations are matched: `sin(k_u a) = 0` makes the window transform vanish at `k_u`, so the first and second field integrals of both profiles vanish, and an on-axis particle exits with zero offset and angle to first order in `K/γ` (in the planar mid-plane, to all orders by symmetry). Helical second-order residuals shrink with longer, more adiabatic ramps. `resonance(scale, γ)` returns `K = e B₀ λ_u / (2π mₑ c)`, `ω_u = 2πc/λ_u`, the on-axis fundamental `2γ²ω_u/(1 + K²/2)` (planar) or `2γ²ω_u/(1 + K²)` (helical), and the wiggler critical frequency `(3/2)γ² K ω_u`. Only odd harmonics radiate on axis in the planar device; the line of harmonic `n` has relative width `≈ 0.886/(nN)`.
- `DipoleBendField(field, length, *, center, fringe_width, aperture)` is a straight-pole dipole with analytic tanh fringes and exact magnetic length `length`; in the mid-plane the exit transverse momentum is `−q B₀ length`. The map frame is not rotated with the orbit.
- `TabulatedFieldMap(x_nodes, y_nodes, z_nodes, magnetic, *, electric=None)` interpolates a static rectilinear table with the native multilinear gather. Positions beyond the longitudinal node range receive zero field with support; positions inside that range but outside the transverse grid are unsupported. `magnetic_interpolation_estimate` (and `electric_…`) report `Σ_i h_i² max|∂_i² f| / 8` per component from second divided differences, the multilinear error bound of a `C²` field.

`FieldMapBeamline(scale, elements, *, element_ids)` superposes elements and binds one `ElectromagneticScaleContract`: every length, time, and field is in that scale's units, and a sample is supported only where every element supports it.

`FieldMapTrackingPlan(beamline, *, entrance_plane, exit_plane, time_step, step_count, method="boris", convention=None, reference_time=0.0, maximum_trajectory_bytes=2**31)` and `track_field_map(plan, bunch)` track in lab time with `RelativisticPushPlan` in the beamline scale. The s ↔ t conversion follows `AcceleratorConvention`: a particle at the entrance plane has momentum `p₀(1 + δ)` along `(px, py, √((1+δ)² − px² − py²))/(1 + δ)` and arrives at `t = reference_time + σ ζ/(β₀c)` with `σ = +1` for `"positive-late"` and `−1` for `"positive-early"`; each lane advances from its own arrival time. At the exit plane the crossing of the exact leapfrog drift is converted back with `ζ = σ β₀c (t_exit − reference_time − (exit − entrance)/(β₀c))` relative to a reference moving at `β₀c` along `z`, so an undulator's path-length excess appears as exit `ζ`. Reference energy, momentum, and charge are in the scale's units; macroparticle charge is `reference_charge`.

`FieldMapTrackingResult` carries the exit-plane `AcceleratorBunch` (lanes that did not exit, left support, or entered invalid are inactive with NaN coordinates), a lab-time `ChargedTrajectory` with per-lane times, node-centered proper velocities and accelerations (one lane per bunch slot, `multiplicities = weights`), and `FieldMapTrackingEvidence`: `FieldMapTrackingStatus` bits (`NONFINITE`, `SUPERLUMINAL`, `UNSUPPORTED`, `NOT_EXITED`, `INVALID_ENTRANCE`), per-lane `entrance_valid`, `supported` (never left field support before the exit plane), `exited`, exit time, position and proper velocity, and the largest sampled `|B|`. A lane that leaves support is deactivated at that node. Recorded trajectory memory is refused above `maximum_trajectory_bytes` with `FieldMapTrackingResourceError`. The trajectory feeds `phydrax.electromagnetics.TrajectoryRadiationPlan` directly; `examples/undulator_radiation.py` recovers the resonant odd harmonics and their `1/(nN)` widths from a tracked electron.

## Storage-ring radiation

`RingLattice(elements, *, periodicity=1)` is one periodic cell of `RingElement(kind, element_id, ...)` values repeated `periodicity` times. `RingElementKind` is `"drift"` (`length`), `"quadrupole"` (`length`, `gradient` `k₁`), `"sector-bend"` (`length`, `curvature` `h = 1/ρ`, optional combined-function `gradient`), or the thin `"rf-cavity"` (`voltage` per charge, `harmonic`); parameters foreign to a kind are refused. Bends are horizontal normal-entry sector bends: edge angles, vertical or skew bends, and nonlinear multipoles cannot be expressed. A lattice without bends, or whose bends do not close (`periodicity · Σ h L ≠ 2π`), raises `RingOpticsError`.

`RingRadiationPlan(lattice, scale, *, reference_rest_energy, reference_momentum, reference_charge, convention=None, spectrum=None, minimum_lorentz_factor=100.0, maximum_quantum_parameter=1e-2)` binds an `ElectromagneticScaleContract` (lengths, energies, charge, and cavity voltages in its units; momentum in its momentum unit) and solves, on the host:

- `optics` (`RingOptics`): first-order canonical maps in `(x, px, y, py, z, δ)` with `z = −β₀cΔt` from the Hill equations `x'' + (h² + k₁)x = hδ`, `y'' − k₁y = 0`, path lengthening `−hx`, and velocity slip `δ/γ₀²`; the periodic Twiss and dispersion at every element boundary, ring tunes by accumulated phase advance, momentum compaction, and slip factor. Unstable or (half-)integer-resonant cell optics are refused.
- `integrals` (`RingRadiationIntegrals`): `I₁ = ∮ηh`, `I₂ = ∮h²`, `I₃ = ∮|h|³`, `I₄ = ∮ηh(h² + 2k₁)`, `I₅ = ∮|h|³ℋ` with `ℋ = γ_xη² + 2α_xηη' + β_xη'²`, integrated per bend with the 15-point Gauss–Kronrod rule on the exact in-bend optics; per-element contributions and the Kronrod–Gauss difference are reported.
- `equilibrium` (`RingRadiationEquilibrium`): damping partition `J = (1 − I₄/I₂, 1, 2 + I₄/I₂)`, energy loss per turn `U₀ = (2/3) r E_rest γ₀⁴ I₂` with `r = q²/(4πε₀E_rest)`, damping times `2E₀T₀/(JᵢU₀)` (also in turns), equilibrium energy spread `σ_δ² = C_qγ₀²I₃/(J_sI₂)` and emittance `ε_x = C_qγ₀²I₅/(J_xI₂)` with `C_q = 55ħc/(32√3 E_rest)`, mean photons per turn, the critical energy, and the quantum parameter `χ = ħcγ₀²|h|/E_rest` of the strongest bend. A ring without a radiation-damped equilibrium (`J_x ≤ 0`, as in combined-function alternating-gradient rings, or `J_s ≤ 0`), a reference below `minimum_lorentz_factor`, and `χ > maximum_quantum_parameter` are refused.
- `one_turn`: the non-radiating first-order one-turn `SymplecticMapPlan` in the bunch convention (RF linearized about its zero-loss phase), usable with `track_ring` and `linear_ring_optics`. `SymplecticMapPlan` checks symplecticity with the convention's form: `δ` is conjugate to the positive-early coordinate, so `"positive-late"` maps carry the opposite longitudinal sign.

`SynchrotronPhotonSpectrum(*, node_count=4097, maximum_fraction=50.0)` tabulates the classical photon-number spectrum `F(ξ)/ξ` of `phydrax.special.synchrotron_f` in `t = ξ^{1/3}` (smooth and finite at the origin) and samples `ξ = u/u_c` by the exact inverse of its piecewise-linear interpolant (`sample_fractions`). It reports the exact moments of the sampled table, direct Gauss–Legendre moments of `F` (the references are `∫F/ξ = 5π/3`, `⟨ξ⟩ = 8/(15√3)`, `⟨ξ²⟩ = 11/27`), and a bound on the omitted tail.

`RadiativeRingTrackingPlan(radiation, turn_count, *, model, horizontal_aperture, vertical_aperture, slices_per_bend=4, photon_overflow_probability=1e-12, maximum_bytes=2**30)` and `track_radiative_ring(plan, bunch, *, key=None)` track an `AcceleratorBunch` (float64, matching reference particle and convention, unique `particle_ids`) through every element map. Bends are split into slices with a radiation kick at each slice center; RF cavities apply `|q|V sin(φ_s − k_rf z)` with `k_rf = 2πh_rf/C`. `RingRadiationModel` selects:

- `"none"`: symplectic tracking; without RF it reproduces `track_ring(plan.radiation.one_turn)`;
- `"classical"`: the deterministic mean loss `(2/3) q²γ₀⁴(1 + δ)²(h + k₁x)²(1 + hx)L/(4πε₀)` per slice (damping without excitation);
- `"stochastic"`: a Poisson photon count of mean `(5/(2√3)) α_q γ₀ |h + k₁x|(1 + hx)L` with energies `ξ u_c`, `u_c = (3/2)ħcγ₀³(1 + δ)²|h + k₁x|`, drawn from the classical spectrum (damping and quantum excitation). Every emission derives its stream with `derive_key(key, address, turn, cell, step, particle_id)`, so results are independent of slot order and batching.

Emission removes momentum `u/c` along the direction of motion (`δ → δ − u/(p₀c)`, `(px, py)` scaled by `1 − u/(p₀c(1 + δ))`); the vertical opening angle is not modeled, so an uncoupled ring equilibrates to zero vertical emittance. The synchronous phase restores the model's reference loss per turn on the stable side of transition; cavities that cannot restore `U₀` and unstable lumped synchrotron motion are refused. The per-slice photon capacity is the smallest whose Poisson overflow probability at the reference rate is at most `photon_overflow_probability`; realized overflows are counted and the run is not accepted. Memory (per-turn moments plus the per-step photon workspace) above `maximum_bytes` raises `RadiativeRingTrackingResourceError`.

`RadiativeRingTrackingResult` carries the final bunch (lost lanes inactive) and `RadiativeRingTrackingEvidence`: `RadiativeRingTrackingStatus` bits (`NONFINITE`, `PHOTON_OVERFLOW`, `PARTICLE_LOSS`), `accepted`, per-turn weighted mean and covariance in the bunch convention, dispersion-free horizontal emittance, vertical emittance, RMS `δ`, mean radiated and RF energy per particle, mean photon count, transmission, per-lane loss turn, and the overflow and nonfinite counts. Emission events are discrete and not differentiable.

## Free-electron lasers

The time-independent period-averaged FEL (`phydrax.applications.accelerator.fel`) builds undulator lattices from `InsertionDeviceField` segments, loads quiet-start beamlets with identity-addressed Fawley shot noise, and integrates seeded or noise-started slices with harmonic `[JJ]_h` coupling, taper, focusing, per-slice wakes, and a particle↔field energy ledger. See [Free-electron lasers](guides_free_electron_lasers.md).

## Interchange

`accelerator_bunch_from_openpmd_columns` admits one explicit normalized openPMD column profile. `write_madx_sequence` writes only its stated drift/quadrupole/horizontal-steerer subset and refuses other elements rather than approximating them.
