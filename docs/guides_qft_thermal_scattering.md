# Thermal, diagrammatic, and scattering QFT

## Discrete Lehmann representations

DLR plans bind inverse temperature, spectral cutoff, tolerance, statistics,
and symmetry. Imaginary-time, Matsubara, and DLR Green functions preserve
matrix axes, high-frequency moments, and representation evidence. Dyson solves
and self-energy extraction use canonical linear algebra.

Analytic continuation is a separate ill-posed inference problem. Pade,
maximum-entropy, and sparse regularized methods report their prior or
regularizer, data covariance, effective resolution, and failure status.

## Diagrammatic field theory and EFT

Typed diagrams record field species, propagators, vertices, external legs,
momentum/frequency routing, spin/color/flavor indices, symmetry factors,
perturbative order, regulator, counterterms, and renormalization scheme. They
lower into the existing computational DAG.

Diagrammatic Monte Carlo carries exact topology/order proposal ratios, sign or
phase, normalization reference, order histogram, autocorrelation, and tail
truncation. EFT declarations separate operator basis, matching, running,
power-counting truncation, regulator variation, and stochastic error.

## Relativistic scattering

Four-momenta, mass shells, frames, particles, spin/polarization bases, and
incoming/outgoing conventions remain explicit. Amplitude tensors precede all
spin/color sums and phase-space integration.

VEGAS uses finite adaptation followed by a frozen grid. Multi-channel maps
carry exact Jacobians and mixture densities. Weighted, signed, and unweighted
events use distinct measures and provenance. Tree-level process claims do not
imply loop or infrared-subtraction support.
