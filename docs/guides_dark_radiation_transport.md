# Dark-radiation propagation and backreaction

The existing dark-radiation ledger is a source record. Propagation uses separate packet,
moment, VET and homogeneous-hierarchy profiles.

## Packet profile

`DarkRadiationPacketState` stores global IDs, species/parents/generation, comoving
position, tetrad four-momentum, frequency group, Stokes/coherency, optical-depth state,
RNG, owner, weight, frame realization and epoch identity. `DarkRadiationPacketPlan`
advances FLRW/geodesic drift, redshift, absorption/scattering/emission/polarization,
lineage and exact weighted four-force with transactional capacity rollback. Deferred
products use the shared durable epoch runtime.

## Multigroup M1

`CosmologicalMultigroupM1System` adds conservative group-redshift flux and exact frame
identity to the existing relativistic radiation owner. `DarkRadiationM1ExchangeResult`
reports positivity, realizability, matter/radiation opposite-sign four-force, physical-c
or separately identified reduced-c policy, AMR reflux and checkpoint evidence. Crossing
beam risk is refused rather than hidden.

## VET and hierarchy

`DarkRadiationVETPlan` owns a fixed angular quadrature formal solve and reports
Eddington-tensor/lag/iteration/shadow evidence. `DarkRadiationBoltzmannHierarchyPlan`
owns fixed scale/k/momentum/multipole/polarization support, metric sources, collisions,
closure and line-of-sight outputs.

Packet-to-moment and moment-to-hierarchy conversion require explicit averaging or
linearization receipts. Every profile returns `StressEnergyProjection` and an equal and
opposite matter four-force for gravity coupling.
