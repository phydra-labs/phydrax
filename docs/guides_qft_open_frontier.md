# Open, semiclassical, and frontier QFT

## Gaussian and atomic systems

Gaussian bipartitions, partial transpose, PPT margins, logarithmic negativity,
and entropy extend the canonical covariance-state substrate. Atomic manifold
compilers use angular-momentum selection rules and lower coherent drives and
radiative channels into existing Hamiltonian, Lindblad, CPTP, and trajectory
solvers.

## Deterministic ensembles and semiclassical QED

Initial-condition ensembles sit above deterministic differential solvers and
remain distinct from stochastic dynamics. Semiclassical QED models carry mode
normalization, gauge, regulator, current subtraction, Maxwell backreaction,
energy/Ward diagnostics, and causal response evidence. Spatial, dimensional,
and subtraction changes are separate support claims.

Classical Maxwell eigenmodes may lower to normalized effective quantum modes,
participation factors, couplings, and Purcell evidence. Classical discretization
error remains independent from quantum-model reduction error.

## Variable sectors and nonequilibrium fields

Variable-particle configurations use a fixed compiled capacity and exact
active mask. Their disjoint-union measure includes sector weights and
identical-particle combinatorics. Birth/death proposals carry exact reverse
densities and Jacobians.

Classical-statistical, Keldysh, and 2PI methods retain initial ensemble,
contour, memory, truncation, causality, and conservation evidence.

## Functional RG and learned acceleration

Functional RG represents finite effective-action truncations and reports
regulator and truncation dependence. Learned proposals, metrics, surrogates,
and coarse spaces may accelerate an exact method only when frozen production
retains exact correction and out-of-domain refusal.

## Complex weights and frontier theories

Reweighting reports average phase, overlap, ratio uncertainty, and effective
sample size. Complex Langevin and holomorphic-flow methods remain research-only
and expose drift-tail, boundary-term, Jacobian, residual-phase, and abstention
diagnostics.

Supersymmetric complexified links are not reunitarized. Anyonic fusion data
must satisfy pentagon and hexagon identities. Conformal-bootstrap results keep
their tensor-basis gauge, spin/derivative/pole/block/precision truncations,
frontend identity, exact PMP artifact, external solver evidence, and
independent sampled audit. A sampled cone or successful external solve is not a
continuum exclusion. Curved-spacetime QFT records the state, background,
subtraction, Bogoliubov, stress-conservation, and backreaction evidence and
makes no quantum-gravity claim.
