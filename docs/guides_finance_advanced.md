# Advanced finance candidates

Advanced finance routes deliberately have a candidate ceiling.

- **Martingale transport** reports finite-grid marginal and martingale defects and bound evidence.
- **Rough methods** identify the kernel representation, law, discretization, and replay assumptions.
- **Deep and operator methods** separate learned-model evidence from numerical-solver evidence and never treat training loss as pricing qualification.
- **Tensor methods** identify representation, truncation, contraction, and residual evidence.

Use `advanced_finance_support` to name one exact route. The builder accepts only `martingale-transport`, `rough`, `deep`, `operator`, or `tensor`, records `live_trading=False`, and rejects any maturity above `candidate`.

Candidate results establish only the finite synthetic or admitted problem that was run. They do not establish continuum convergence, all-market completeness, vendor parity, executable hedging, capacity at an unmeasured scale, live deployment, or regulatory fitness. Graduation requires a new exact support tuple and independent data, model, numerical, and use evidence; it cannot be inferred from benchmark timing.
